Design a premium, dark, rebellious e-commerce homepage for a modern Indian fragrance brand called "NO SAINT".

BRAND:
NO SAINT
Tagline: "SMELL GOOD. MISBEHAVE."

NO SAINT is a contemporary perfume brand targeted at men and women aged 18–35 in India.

The brand should feel premium and aspirational but NOT traditional luxury. It should feel youthful, provocative, confident, mysterious, sensual and slightly rebellious.

The core brand idea is:
"You don't wear perfume to behave. You wear it to be remembered."

The visual identity must communicate attitude and individuality rather than traditional luxury.

Use an 8px spacing system.
Use generous whitespace.
Use consistent 12-column desktop grid.
Maximum content width approximately 1200–1280px.
Use large 48–96px editorial headlines on desktop.
Body text should remain highly readable.
Buttons should be clean rectangular or subtly rounded, not pill-shaped.
Use thin borders and subtle separators.
Use hover states where appropriate.

BUTTON STYLE:
Primary buttons:
Off-white background with black text.

Transparent background with off-white text and subtle off-white border.

Blood red should be reserved for:
- Small highlights
- Hover states
- Dividers
- Important labels
- Small decorative elements
- Selected states
